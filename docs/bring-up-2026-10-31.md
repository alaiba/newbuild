## Physical build decisions and wiring

- CPU cooler: **Thermalright Phantom Spirit 120** with supplied TF7 paste, AMD mounting bars and two 120 mm fans. User chose **middle + rear** fan placement to keep the area above the low-profile Crucial RAM clear; intended airflow goes toward the rear exhaust.
- Both cooler fans attach via the Thermalright **PWM Y-splitter** to motherboard `CPU_FAN` (one fan tachometer line is normal). The fan wires were secured to the case's upper crossbar; inspect any remaining slack near fan blades.
- The be quiet! case's rear exhaust fan is on `CHA_FAN1`; front intake on `CHA_FAN2`. Both headers show plausible RPM.
- 24-pin motherboard power, CPU EPS power, `F_PANEL`, front USB-A, front USB-C and front `HD_AUDIO` connected. Cable runs behind the motherboard tray secured with straps; actual front-panel port tests remain outstanding.
- Unused PSU `VGA`/PCIe power leads are reserved for the RTX 3060; do not connect them to motherboard power headers.
- The first boot showed AMD's new-CPU/fTPM initialization message, then the machine reached UEFI. The exact fTPM reset choice/state was not independently documented; do not infer that any older disk encryption keys are available.
